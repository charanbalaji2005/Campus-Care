### CampusCare v1.3.0 Release Notes

#### 🌟 What is New

1. **Complete Campus Admin Portal**:
   - Unified operational view across Security, Committee, Counselling, and Student workflows.
   - 12 production screens: Dashboard, Live Campus Overview, SOS Alerts & Security Oversight, Incident Reports & Committee Oversight, Counselling Overview, Student Management, Security Management, Committee Management, Reports & Analytics, Announcements, Messages & Communication, and Profile & Settings.
   - 5-tab persistent bottom navigation bar (Home, Security, Counselling, Reports, More) with central SOS button removed for staff.
   - Dedicated "More" hub for streamlined navigation across all administrative and operational modules.
   - Campus-scoped access control ensuring administrators can only access and manage records within their designated campus.

2. **Counsellor Portal (11 Production Screens)**:
   - Full mental health and student wellness support portal for verified counsellors.
   - Appointment scheduling, requests queue, student profile history, online counselling sessions, session notes editor, case management, resources library, and analytics.
   - Emergency escalation workflow with confirmation dialog for high-risk situations.
   - Strict privacy guarantees: confidential session notes, clinical disclosures, and student identities are protected and inaccessible to unauthorized roles.

3. **Security Officer & Committee Member Portals with Strict Incident Routing**:
   - **Student SOS → Security Officers ONLY**: Emergency alerts route exclusively to on-duty security responders with live maps, responder status updates, patrol tracking, and complete timeline audit trails.
   - **Student Incident Reports → Committee Members ONLY**: Formal reports route directly to designated committee queues for investigation, evidence handling, and resolution tracking.
   - Absolute workflow separation: Security officers never receive committee incident reports, and committee members never receive active SOS alerts.

4. **Identity Verification & Staff Registration**:
   - Streamlined staff registration and application workflow for university personnel.
   - Document evidence upload and verification recommendations reviewed by campus admins and approved by super admins.
